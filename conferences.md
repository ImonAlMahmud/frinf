# Conferences & Events — Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation

Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation convenes national research symposiums, public health conferences, and practical capacity building workshops designed to foster interdisciplinary collaboration.

## Flagship Event: International Public Health & Research Symposium 2026
- **Date**: October 24–25, 2026
- **Location**: Dhaka, Bangladesh
- **Theme**: "Resilient Health Systems & Evidence-Based Community Innovation"
- **Overview**: Convenes 400+ researchers, clinicians, public health experts, and policymakers for 2 days of keynote presentations, panel debates, poster sessions, and grant announcements.

## Event Categories
1. **Academic Symposiums**: National conferences on genomics, infectious disease surveillance, and biostatistics.
2. **Methodology Workshops**: Hands-on training on R programming, systematic reviews, and manuscript writing.
3. **Policy Roundtables**: Expert dialogue linking empirical research to national health policy implementation.
