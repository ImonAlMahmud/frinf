# Membership & Fellowship — Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation

Membership connects researchers, doctors, students, and social innovators across Bangladesh and internationally to collaborate, vote on foundation priorities, and access grant funding.

## Membership Categories

### 1. Student Associate
- **Eligibility**: Undergraduate & graduate students.
- **Fee**: BDT 1,500 / year
- **Benefits**: Access to methodology workshops, paper submission discounts, mentorship program, digital certificate.

### 2. Scholar Member
- **Eligibility**: Postgraduates, PhD candidates, university faculty & medical professionals.
- **Fee**: BDT 3,500 / year
- **Benefits**: Seed grant eligibility, peer-review panel inclusion, priority conference registration, voting rights.

### 3. Institutional Fellow
- **Eligibility**: Senior academic leaders, department chairs, and NGO directors.
- **Fee**: BDT 10,000 / year
- **Benefits**: Board meeting invitations, institutional partner branding, advisory committee role, grant review panel leadership.
