# Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation

> Knowledge that heals, empowers & transforms.

Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation is a non-government, non-political, non-profit organization dedicated to research, education, training, public health, social development, and humanitarian action across Bangladesh.

## Vision Statement
To build a healthier, knowledgeable, skilled, and socially developed society through research, innovation, education, and community empowerment.

## Core Pillars
1. **Scientific & Medical Research**: Epidemiological field studies, seed grants, open-access publications.
2. **Education & Professional Training**: Biostatistics, data science, scientific writing workshops.
3. **Public Health & Prevention**: Free medical clinics, maternal care, hygiene drives.
4. **Social Development**: Youth skill incubation, gender equity, community libraries.
5. **Humanitarian Emergency Relief**: Rapid flood response, medical aid, rehabilitation.
6. **Institutional Partnerships**: Academic MoU collaborations, sustainable infrastructure.
