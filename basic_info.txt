About Us

Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation is a non-government, non-political, non-profit organization dedicated to research, education, training, public health, social development, and humanitarian activities.

Our Vision

To build a healthier, knowledgeable, skilled, and socially developed society through research, innovation, education, and community empowerment.

Our Mission

To promote research, education, healthcare, skills development, and social welfare while creating sustainable opportunities for vulnerable communities and contributing to national development.
