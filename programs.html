Core Domain Programs

PILLAR 01

Scientific & Medical Research

Conducting ethical, rigorous investigations in epidemiology, public health policy, and environmental health across Bangladesh.

  • Young Investigator Seed Grants
  • Epidemiological Field Studies
  • Open Access Journal Publication
PILLAR 02

Education & Professional Training

Capacity building through specialized training workshops, research methodology seminars, and scholar mentorship.

  • Biostatistics & Data Science Courses
  • Scientific Writing Workshops
  • Student Fellowships
PILLAR 03

Public Health & Prevention

Direct healthcare screening camps, maternal awareness campaigns, and disease prevention initiatives in rural districts.

  • Rural Free Medical Clinics
  • Maternal & Neonatal Health Care
  • Hygiene & Sanitation Drives
PILLAR 04

Social Development

Targeted programs addressing social inequalities, youth employment readiness, and digital literacy in rural communities.

  • Youth Skill Incubation
  • Gender Equity Workshops
  • Community Libraries
PILLAR 05

Humanitarian Emergency Relief

Rapid deployment of medical kits, food packages, and clean water supplies during floods, cyclones, and health crises.

  • Flood & Cyclone Rapid Response
  • Emergency Medical Aid
  • Post-Disaster Rehabilitation
PILLAR 06

Institutional Partnerships

Grant support and collaborative MoUs for climate adaptation, scientific publication, and local health governance.

  • Academic MoU Collaborations
  • Clean Water Infrastructure
  • Grassroots Leadership Support
