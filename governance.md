# Institutional Governance — Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation

Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation maintains a robust, checks-and-balances organizational model to ensure independent scientific inquiry, ethical stewardship, and financial transparency.

## Organizational Structure
1. **General Advisory Council**: Distinguished scholars, university deans, and senior public health directors providing long-term strategic guidance.
2. **Board of Trustees**: Governing body responsible for fiduciary oversight, statutory compliance, and annual budget approvals.
3. **Executive Secretariat**: Full-time operational leadership managing research grants, community empowerment programs, and daily activities.

## Ethical Standards & Principles
- **Non-Political & Non-Sectarian Charter**: Strictly non-partisan, dedicated solely to scientific research, public health, and social development.
- **Peer Review Oversight**: All research grant awards undergo double-blind peer review by external subject experts.
- **Financial Audit & Transparency**: Annual independent financial audits published for donor and public review.
