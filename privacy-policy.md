# Privacy Policy — Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation

*Effective Date: January 1, 2026*

Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation is committed to maintaining the highest ethical standards regarding personal privacy and data security. We process all personal information in accordance with applicable national laws in Bangladesh and internationally recognized data privacy principles.

## 1. Zero Commercialization
We never sell, rent, monetize, or commercialize any personal information submitted by our scholars, donors, event attendees, or community beneficiaries.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Membership & Account Information**: Name, institutional affiliation, academic designation, professional email, phone number, and research area.
- **Donation Records**: Transaction IDs, contribution amounts, contact details (payment credentials are handled by secure processors and never stored).
- **Grant Submissions**: Proposals, CVs, and recommendation letters.
- **Technical Log Data**: Anonymized IP logs and performance metrics.

## 3. Data Protection Rights
You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data stored in our foundation records.
