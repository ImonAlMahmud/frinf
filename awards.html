Recognizing Innovation

Honoring academic excellence & funding frontier research.

Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation annually confers prestige awards and seed funding to researchers, medical pioneers, and community advocates making transformative contributions in Bangladesh.

Grants & Honors

Annual Award Categories

Foundation Research Excellence Award

Conferred annually to senior scholars for landmark published research in public health, infectious diseases, or social development.

Honorarium: BDT 100,000 + Gold Medal

Young Investigator Seed Grant

Competitive seed funding for early-career researchers under 35 conducting pilot studies in community health and innovation.

Grant: Up to BDT 200,000 per project

Community Service Medal

Honoring grassroots leaders, field doctors, and volunteers who demonstrate exceptional dedication during humanitarian crises.

Honorarium: BDT 50,000 + Citation
