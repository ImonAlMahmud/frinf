Frontier Research & Innovation Foundation

Dedicated to research, education & humanitarian action.

Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation is a non-government, non-political, non-profit organization dedicated to research, education, training, public health, social development, and humanitarian activities.

Our Origins

Fostering progress through evidence & empathy.

Founded by a collective of researchers, medical experts, educators, and development practitioners, the foundation was established to bridge the gap between academic research and grassroots execution in Bangladesh.

We believe sustainable societal transformation requires an integrated ecosystem: rigorous research informs policy, education builds human capital, public health ensures wellbeing, and humanitarian relief safeguards dignity during crisis.

Key Organizational Pillars

  • Interdisciplinary Research

    Supporting high-impact investigations in public health, social sciences, and technology innovation.

  • Human Capital Development

    Training youth, scholars, and healthcare workers through accredited workshops and fellowships.

  • Humanitarian Support

    Providing direct emergency aid and community empowerment programs to vulnerable populations.

Guiding Philosophy

Our Core Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To build a healthier, knowledgeable, skilled, and socially developed society through research, innovation, education, and community empowerment.

Long-term societal health & technical self-reliance.

Our Mission

To promote research, education, healthcare, skills development, and social welfare while creating sustainable opportunities for vulnerable communities and contributing to national development.

Direct community service backed by scientific evidence.
Ethical Foundation

Values that steer our foundation.

Integrity & Ethics

Innovation & Discovery

Our Journey

Key Milestones & History

2024 — Incorporation & Charter

Foundation Charter Established

Formally established in Dhaka as a non-government, non-profit foundation under national non-profit regulations, forming its initial 3-tier executive council.

2025 — Public Health & Research Expansion

Nationwide Health & Research Initiative

Launched first 12 rural health awareness camps, provided 5 seed research grants to young investigators, and hosted the inaugural Research Symposium.

2026 — Disaster Relief & Global Partnerships

Humanitarian Relief & Academic Network

Provided emergency flood relief to over 15,000 families while formalizing academic partnerships for public health research.
