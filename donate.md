# Donate & Support — Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation

Every contribution to Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation directly funds health screening camps, student research grants, vocational training, and immediate humanitarian emergency relief in Bangladesh.

## Impact Allocation
- **85% Direct Community Programs**: Medicines, diagnostic kits, flood relief packages, clean water tube wells, and rural health camps.
- **10% Seed Research Grants**: Direct grants awarded to young researchers for local epidemiological and social studies.
- **5% Operations & Governance**: Administration, transparent financial audits, and platform maintenance.
