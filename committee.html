Governance & Leadership

Guiding our foundation with vision, accountability & expertise.

The foundation is governed by a three-tier executive council comprising academic leaders, medical experts, social scientists, and administrative directors committed to non-profit stewardship.

Governance Model

Three-Tier Organizational Structure

1. Governing Body

Provides overarching legal, financial, and strategic oversight. Ensures strict compliance with non-profit mandates and ethical guidelines.

2. Advisory Council

A panel of senior researchers, university professors, and medical experts reviewing grant awards, scientific rigor, and academic standards.

3. Working Secretariat

Executes daily foundation operations, manages field interventions, coordinates conferences, and maintains donor communications.
