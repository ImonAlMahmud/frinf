# Grants & Awards — Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation

Frontier Research and Innovation Foundation annually confers prestige awards and seed funding to researchers, medical pioneers, and community advocates making transformative contributions in Bangladesh.

## Annual Award Categories

### 1. Foundation Research Excellence Award
Conferred annually to senior scholars for landmark published research in public health, infectious diseases, or social development.
- **Honorarium**: BDT 100,000 + Gold Medal

### 2. Young Scientist Seed Grant
Competitive seed grant awarded to early-career researchers under 35 years old to initiate novel pilot studies.
- **Grant Amount**: Up to BDT 250,000 per project

### 3. Community Impact Fellowship
Recognizes community healthcare workers, field coordinators, and social activists delivering frontline health relief.
- **Award**: Grant Certificate + Institutional Mentorship
